Interactive Installation
2015. Presented at Spark! Mesa's Festival of Creativity.

Tools: Custom software, Microsoft Kinect, Monitor.
Collaboration with David Tinapple.

FaceFlip! was presented at Spark! Mesa's Festival of Creativity in collaboration with David Tinapple. The piece uses Kinect depth sensing to detect and playfully transform visitors' faces in real time.
