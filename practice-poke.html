Interactive Sculpture
2015

Tools: Custom Software, Adobe After Effects, Infrared Camera, Projector, Spandex screen, Wooden structure, Metal stand.

A playful interactive sculpture exploring touch and response. A tensioned Spandex surface acts as both a physical membrane and projection screen. When visitors touch or poke the surface, the projected imagery responds dynamically, blurring the boundary between the physical and the digital.
