Interactive Sculpture
2015

Tools: Custom Software, Infrared Camera, Microphone, CorelDraw, Mylar film, Laser cut wood.

A generative audio-visual sculpture responding to environmental sound. Thin Mylar surfaces suspended in a laser-cut wood structure flutter and shimmer in response to sound captured by a microphone, while projected imagery reacts to the same audio input.
