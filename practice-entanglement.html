3D Animation and Printed Object
2015

Tools: Motion Capture, MotionBuilder, Maya, Zcorp 3D printer.

An exploration of entangled bodies through motion capture and 3D printing. The work captures the complex, intertwined motion of figures and renders it as a physical object, making visible the relationships that exist between bodies in motion.
