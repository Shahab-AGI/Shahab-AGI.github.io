Interactive Installation
Presented at Harry Wood Gallery, 2017, Tempe, AZ.
Currents New Media, 2017, Santa Fe, NM.

An interactive installation exploring the gestures embedded in everyday routines. The work investigates how mundane physical movements carry meaning and how those gestures can be recontextualized through interactive technology.

Tools: Adobe After Effects, Premiere, SolidWorks, custom tracking software.
