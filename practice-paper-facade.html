Interactive Sculpture
2016

Tools: Custom software, Adobe After Effects, Illustrator, CorelDraw, Green screen footage, Wooden frame, Laser cut Matboard, Monitor, Contact microphone.

An interactive sculpture investigating surface, transparency, and projected image. The piece responds to sound captured through a contact microphone, animating laser-cut paper surfaces with projected imagery to reveal layered meanings.
